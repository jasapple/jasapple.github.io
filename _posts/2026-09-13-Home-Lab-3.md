## Networking

I'm keeping the Dream Machine SE as my controller, but I've added the following for switching capacity:

- USW Aggregation
- USW Pro 24 PoE
- USW Ultra
- USW Flex Mini

Most devices are connected to the Pro 24 aside from the NAS and Ark which have 10G networking. The Aggregation is connected via its 2 10G SFP+ ports to the 24 Pro. The Ultra is powered by the 24 Pro via PoE++ which then also powers the USW Flex Mini which is used in my media center for all my devices there. The only device not in the 24 Pro is the pihole which is connected and powered by the Dream Machine
